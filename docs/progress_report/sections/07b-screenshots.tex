\roundthree{Figure~\ref{fig:ui-pwa} follows one new paid member on a phone. The person registers with the Authorization Number, a password, a photo, and the waiver (a--f), then signs in (g) and opens Check In (i). After Check In, the Gym ID shows the name, the photo, the authorization, and the Check In time (k), and the occupancy count includes the person (m). Figure~\ref{fig:ui-desk} shows the front desk: the person appears in Sesiones activas (a), and Staff open the session (b) and check the person out, so the visit ends on the phone (Figure~\ref{fig:ui-pwa}n) and the occupancy goes down. Demo videos of these flows will be on the project website, \url{https://th3-minorities.github.io/natatorio-gym-app/}.}
