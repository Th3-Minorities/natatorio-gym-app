\textbf{Stack.} & Laravel and PostgreSQL in docker-compose replace Supabase and Vercel. & Solution Approach; Technical Approach \\
\addlinespace
\textbf{Hosting.} & Docker Desktop on each PC in development; a CTI VM (Rocky Linux 10) with a UPRM subdomain and HTTPS in production, with backups on a second disk. & Solution Approach \\
\addlinespace
\textbf{Live updates.} & Polling every \roundthree{25 seconds} replaces real-time channels. & Technical Approach \\
\addlinespace
\textbf{Accounts.} & Own accounts with email and password replace institutional login, one device per User. Staff and Administrators have individual accounts, invited by email, and the first Administrator comes from the install command. & Solution Approach; Technical Approach \\
\addlinespace
\textbf{Registration.} & One field: a 9-digit number is a student number, anything else is an Authorization Number. Paid memberships come from the store's API with a read-only key, and only completed orders count. Athletes register from a link sent to their roster email. & Solution Approach \\
\addlinespace
\textbf{Visits.} & A visit stays open until Staff check the person out, with an automatic close 2 hours after Check In. Athletes-only periods show a notice and never end a visit. & Solution Approach; Objectives \\
\addlinespace
\textbf{Safety records.} & The system keeps the waiver acceptance with its version and an emergency contact. & Solution Approach \\
\addlinespace
\textbf{Access rules.} & Administrators grant or revoke an authorization by hand with a logged reason. Check In accepts a browser location reading with accuracy of 50 m or better, within 50 m of the gym. & Solution Approach; Technical Approach \\
\addlinespace
\textbf{Devices.} & The User app is for phones; the Staff and Administrator dashboard is for desktop. & Technical Approach, User Interface Design \\
\addlinespace
\textbf{Analytics.} & Open to every signed-in role, with aggregated counts only. & Objectives \\
\addlinespace
\textbf{Data.} & Visit data is kept 5 years; only the current photo is kept; daily totals with no personal data are kept for good. & Solution Approach \\
